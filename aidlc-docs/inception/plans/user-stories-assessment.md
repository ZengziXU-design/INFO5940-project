# User Stories Assessment

## Request analysis

- **Original request:** Complete the CourseCompass Inception milestone through Units Generation. The team approved the two input documents and Requirements Analysis at revision e45ec09.
- **User impact:** Direct. Students use a conversational interface to capture preferences, verify their input, plan and compare schedules, and refine results.
- **Complexity:** Complex. Each recommendation must preserve confirmed constraints, handle uncertain course data, and distinguish infeasibility from incomplete search or unavailable data.
- **Stakeholders:** Cornell students across majors and degree programs, and the INFO 5940 team members who will review, build, and verify the MVP.

## Criteria met

- [x] High priority. New user-facing features, different student planning goals, and complex scheduling and exception behavior.
- [x] Additional context. The workflow spans data intake, preference confirmation, scheduling, presentation, and revision. Verification must cover routine paths and failure paths.
- [x] Expected benefits. Stories will map the approved requirements to observable outcomes so the team can review what a student can do and verify it in Construction.

## Decision

Execute User Stories. The course milestone requires stories and personas, and the project meets the official rule's high-priority criteria. Prepare the story plan now in the course and core-workflow sequence. The later Workflow Planning stage will record this stage in the overall execution plan.

## Expected outcomes

- Clearly differentiated planning goals with examples for Cornell students across majors.
- Small stories covering the approved F1–F8 scope, linked to requirements and personas.
- Testable acceptance criteria for the available-data path, ambiguous constraints, no feasible schedule, and the labeled local demonstration path.
